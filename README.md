## Installation

1. Download `ovebotai.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/ovebotai/prestashop-module/releases/latest) (under **Assets** - not the *Source code* archives).
2. Unzip it into the `modules` folder in the root of your PrestaShop installation. The archive already contains a ready-made `ovebotai` folder, so you end up with `modules/ovebotai/ovebotai.php`.
3. In the back office, go to **Modules > Module Manager**, find **Ovebot - AI Chatbot, Live Chat & AI Sales Agent** and click **Install**.

Then open the module either from its new menu entry, **Customer Service > Ovebot AI**, or with the **Configure** button on the module card.

The connection uses **OAuth** (authorization code with PKCE) - there are no API keys to copy and paste by hand.

## Initial setup (wizard)

The first time you open the module, a 4-step wizard connects your store to your Ovebot.ai account.

### Step 1 - Connect account

Log in to your Ovebot.ai account, or start on the free plan. You'll be sent to ovebot.ai to sign in, then brought straight back to the back office.

### Step 2 - Website pages

Pick the **CMS pages** (About Us, Delivery, Returns, Terms & Conditions, etc.) your AI agent should read and answer from. Only enabled CMS pages of the current shop are listed.

### Step 3 - Products

Choose how the agent learns your catalog:

- **Use the built-in feed** - the module tells Ovebot.ai the URL of its live product feed. Only products that can be ordered right now (in stock, or out of stock with backorders allowed) are included, and the wizard shows you how many products will be sent.
- **I'll provide my own feed** - keep using a compatible feed you already have (e.g. Google Merchant), configured directly in your Ovebot.ai account.

### Step 4 - Go live

The selected pages and the feed settings are sent to Ovebot.ai and the AI agent goes live on your site immediately. A link to the agent's settings on Ovebot.ai is shown in case you want to fine-tune it.

## Dashboard

Once setup is complete, the module's main screen becomes a dashboard showing your connection status, quick links to chat with the agent or open your Ovebot.ai account, the number of products indexed by the AI, and your synced knowledge bases - each with a direct link to edit the entry on Ovebot.ai, so managing your resources stays one click away. ## Settings

Open **Settings** from the dashboard header.

**On-site chat widget**

- **Show the chat bubble on your site** - enables or disables the widget on the storefront without disconnecting anything.

**Product feed**

- **Feed URL** - the live feed Ovebot.ai reads periodically, with a **Copy** button.
- **Regenerate hash** - invalidates the current feed URL and issues a new one (the change is pushed to Ovebot.ai when connected).
- **Recommend products** - whether the AI agent recommends products from your catalog.
- **Use the built-in product feed** - switch it off if you provide your own feed; the built-in URL then returns *Forbidden*.

**Order tracking API**

- **Enable order tracking** - lets the AI agent look up order status and tracking numbers.
- **Endpoint URL**, **API user**, **API password** - the credentials Ovebot.ai uses to call your store; they are synced automatically when you connect.
- **Regenerate credentials** - issues a new user/password pair; the old ones stop working immediately.

**Appearance**

- **Accent colour** and **Theme** (default, light or dark)
- **Language** - default, *Auto (browser)*, or a fixed language: English, Română, Deutsch, Français or Español
- **Audio beep** - default, play or none
- **Widget position** - bottom right or bottom left, with the **offset from bottom** and **offset from the side** in pixels (raise them to sit above another floating button, e.g. WhatsApp)

## Multistore

Ovebot.ai connects each shop separately, with its own agent, feed URL, order-tracking credentials and widget settings. Switch to a single shop in the back-office shop selector before opening the module; in the **All shops** context the page simply asks you to pick a shop.

## What the module exposes

All endpoints are ordinary module front controllers, so they work with or without friendly URLs.

### Product feed

```
GET index.php?fc=module&module=ovebotai&controller=feed&id_lang=N&hash=…
```

Returns the catalog of the current shop as JSON. Only enabled, orderable products with a price greater than zero are listed; out-of-stock products are excluded unless backorders are allowed for them. The feed is served live, so stock, price and availability changes are reflected on the next read. The `hash` is the per-shop secret from the settings page; a wrong hash, or the built-in feed being switched off, returns `403 Forbidden`.

### Order lookup

```
POST index.php?fc=module&module=ovebotai&controller=orders&id_lang=N
Authorization: Basic <API user>:<API password>
Content-Type: application/json
```

Request body - the order ID or reference, plus the customer's e-mail or phone number:

```json
{"id": "123", "email": "customer@example.com"}
{"id": "XKBKNABJK", "email": "customer@example.com"}
{"id": "123", "phone": "+40 7xx xxx xxx"}
```

Response:

```json
{"success": true, "data": {"id": 123, "reference": "XKBKNABJK", "date": "2026-09-20 10:15:00", "status": "Shipped", "total": 199.9, "currency": "RON", "carrier": "Sameday", "awb": "1ONB…", "awb_tracking_url": "https://…"}}
{"success": false, "error": "…"}
```

Rules:

- The e-mail or phone must match the order's customer; orders older than **60 days** are not returned.
- Authentication is **HTTP Basic** with the API user / password from the settings page.
- **Rate limiting** - 10 failed logins from the same IP within an hour block that IP for an hour (`429`, `Retry-After: 3600`).

### OAuth callback

```
index.php?fc=module&module=ovebotai&controller=oauth
```

Used only during the wizard's **Connect account** step to complete the OAuth flow and return you to the back office.

## Server note - HTTP Basic behind Apache + PHP-FPM/CGI

When PHP runs as FPM or CGI, Apache does not pass the `Authorization` header to PHP by default, so the order lookup endpoint would always answer `401`. Add this to your `.htaccess` (or virtual host):

```apache
SetEnvIf Authorization "(.*)" HTTP_AUTHORIZATION=$1
```

On Apache 2.4.13+ you can use `CGIPassAuth On` instead. The module reads both `HTTP_AUTHORIZATION` and `REDIRECT_HTTP_AUTHORIZATION`.

## Tracking numbers

For each order the module first reads PrestaShop's own tracking number (the order carrier's `tracking_number`). If that is empty, it looks into the tables of these courier modules when they are installed: **Sameday**, **FAN Courier**, **Cargus**, **DPD**, **GLS** and **FedEx**. The table and column names are candidates verified at runtime against the database (they differ between module versions), so a missing or differently named table is skipped silently.

Advanced options (no UI - set them in `ps_configuration`):

| Key | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `OVEBOTAI_TRACKING_FINDERS` | JSON list of finder codes to restrict the lookup to, e.g. `["native","sameday"]`. Codes: `native`, `sameday`, `fancourier`, `cargus`, `dpd`, `gls`, `fedex`. |
| `OVEBOTAI_TRACKING_URLS` | JSON map of finder code to public tracking URL template, `{code}` being replaced by the AWB, e.g. `{"sameday":"https://sameday.ro/#awb={code}"}`. |
| `OVEBOTAI_ORDER_MAX_AGE_DAYS` | Maximum age of orders the lookup endpoint returns (default `60`). |

Developers can plug in their own courier through the `actionOvebotaiTrackingFinders` hook. It receives `finders` (an array, by reference, of objects implementing `Ovebotai\Tracking\TrackingFinderInterface`) and `id_shop`; append or replace finders as needed.

## Knowledge base

The CMS pages you select in the wizard are synced to Ovebot.ai as knowledge base entries with the slug `cms-{id_cms}`; each entry holds the page title followed by its content converted to plain text, plus the page URL as source. From then on:

- editing a selected CMS page in PrestaShop resyncs its entry automatically (`actionObjectCmsUpdateAfter`);
- disabling or deleting it deactivates the entry on Ovebot.ai (`actionObjectCmsDeleteAfter`);
- pages with less than 10 characters of text are skipped, and pages beyond your plan's knowledge base limit are reported as skipped in the wizard.

You can still edit any entry directly on Ovebot.ai from the dashboard's **Edit on Ovebot.ai** links, and add entries from URLs or documents in your account.

## Storefront

The chat widget is injected through the `actionFrontControllerSetMedia` hook: the module pushes the widget configuration to the `ovebot_ai` queue and loads `chat-loader.js` from your workspace's `{workspace}.ovebot.ai` domain. On the order confirmation page it also emits a purchase event with the order details, once per order, so Ovebot.ai can link the order to the conversation that produced it.

## Privacy

- The module contacts Ovebot.ai only after the merchant clicks **Connect** in the wizard; nothing is sent before that.
- Storefront visitors' browsers load the widget from Ovebot.ai; the widget's own cookies and data handling are described in the GDPR section above and in your Ovebot.ai account.
- IP addresses kept for rate limiting of the order lookup endpoint are stored hashed and expire after an hour.
- The module's logs never contain access tokens, API credentials or personal data.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Ovebot cost anything to try?**
No. The free plan is free forever and includes 100 AI replies per month, with no credit card and no time limit. Early access: the free plan is available to the first 200 stores. Paid plans with higher limits are available anytime at ovebot.ai.

**Do I need an Ovebot.ai account?**
Yes, but you can create one without leaving PrestaShop. The setup wizard lets you start on the free plan directly - no credit card, no separate signup on the website.

**How is this different from a normal live chat module?**
A normal live chat waits for you to be online. Ovebot's AI agent answers on its own - from your real product catalog and your own pages - 24/7, and only pulls in a human when the conversation actually needs one.

**Where does the AI get its answers from?**
Only from your data: your PrestaShop catalog, your knowledge base (CMS pages, imported URLs, uploaded documents) and your Agent Memory. It doesn't browse the web and invent answers.

**Can the AI recommend products that are out of stock?**
No. When a product runs out of stock or drops out of your feed, Ovebot removes it automatically, so it stops appearing in chat search and recommendations. If it comes back in stock, it reappears.

**Can my team take over a conversation from the AI?**
Yes, from your Ovebot.ai account. Click **Take Over** on any session, reply directly, then click **Leave** and the AI resumes automatically.

**What happens when I hit my monthly message limit?**
The chat stays on your site and switches to contact-collection mode: visitors leave their details and every submission lands in your Requests inbox. Only the AI's replies count toward your limit - visitor messages and your team's manual replies are free.

**Can I try it before installing?**
Yes. Paste your website URL at [demo.ovebot.ai](https://demo.ovebot.ai) and about a minute later you'll be chatting with an AI agent trained on your own site content. No signup required.

**Does it work with multiple shops?**
Yes. Each shop in a multistore installation connects separately and gets its own agent, feed and settings.
